# ARIA — Phase 4: AI Architecture

## Step 2 — AI Capability Map

**Status:** Draft v1

## Purpose

This document catalogs every AI capability in ARIA, identifies whether it belongs to R0 or a future release, and defines the boundaries of AI responsibility.

---

# R0 AI Capabilities

## Study Intelligence
- Explain concepts
- Answer grounded questions
- Generate examples
- Adjust explanation depth

## Assessment Intelligence
- Generate assessments from approved resources
- Produce explanations for answers
- Support multiple assessment formats

## Resource Intelligence
- Summarize uploaded resources
- Highlight important concepts
- Answer questions with citations

## Adaptation Intelligence
- Recommend the next study focus based on learner state
- Suggest targeted revision topics

---

# Future AI Capabilities

- Personalized roadmap generation
- Planner optimization
- Notes generation
- Audio learning companion
- Career guidance
- Multi-goal coordination
- AI mentor conversations
- Team learning support

---

# Capability Classification

Each capability should specify:

- Purpose
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Required grounding
- Deterministic validation points
- Failure behavior

---

# Capability Boundaries

AI capabilities must never:

- Modify learner state directly
- Invent evidence
- Bypass workflow validation
- Operate outside architectural principles established in Phases 3 and 4

---

# Acceptance Criteria

- R0 capabilities are clearly identified.
- Future capabilities are separated from R0.
- AI responsibilities remain bounded.
- Capability map aligns with Vision, PRD and System Architecture.
